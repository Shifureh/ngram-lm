The old ship creaked as it sailed into the harbor at dawn. Captain Reyes stood on the deck, watching the waves crash against the rocks below.
 
"We can't stop now," he shouted to the crew. "Not when we're this close!"
 
The men didn't hesitate. They'd sailed through worse storms than this one, and they wouldn't let a little rain slow them down. By noon, the ship had reached the port, and the townspeople cheered as it arrived.
 
It was 1847, and news like this traveled fast. Within hours, everyone knew the ship had returned.
