Hello, my name is Henry. I live in a small town near the river. Every morning, I walk to the market to buy bread and cheese.
 
"Good morning, Henry!" said the baker. "Will you have your usual loaf?"
 
"Yes, please," Henry replied, smiling. "And maybe a slice of that cheese, too."
 
The town was quiet, but Henry liked it that way. He had lived there for twenty-three years, and he knew every street, every shop, and every neighbor by name. Life wasn't exciting, but it was good enough.
